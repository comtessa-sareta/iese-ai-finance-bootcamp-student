# Memo house style

1. The headline states the stance in its first five words; no hedging openers.
2. Every figure carries its period and its currency; unitless measures say so.
3. A forecast is never quoted without its measured error, in the same sentence.
4. One sentence of risk per key point — the strongest counter-argument, not a
   disclaimer.
5. Close with "What would change this view": one sentence, falsifiable.
